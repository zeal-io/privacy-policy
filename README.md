# Privacy Policy

> Single-source repository for the Zeal IO Ltd. privacy policy text (`n_pp.txt`) that governs the Zeal Rewards website and mobile applications.

## Contents

`n_pp.txt` - the full privacy policy: consent, what information is collected, who has access, and the purposes of processing for Zeal Rewards users.

## How it is used

The text is the canonical copy that gets published to the website/app legal pages. To change the policy: edit `n_pp.txt`, get the required sign-off, open a PR, and propagate the published copy from this source.
